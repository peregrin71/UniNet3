# ADDENDUM: Causal-Consistency and Non-Overlapping Cone Theorem

## Tier 1D: Causal-Consistency Under Non-Injective Observation

### Theorem 1D.1 — Non-Overlapping Causal Cone Consistency

**Statement.**
In a unitary, local update framework with non-injective observational projection, multiple mutually incompatible macroscopic descriptions may be assigned to the *same deterministic micro-history*, provided that the causal cones induced by those descriptions do not overlap.

**Equivalently:**
> One may alter the observer-level partition of micro-histories into equivalence classes without physical contradiction, so long as no incompatible boundary records are propagated into a shared causal future.

---

### Structural Basis
This theorem follows directly from the following established UniNet results:

1. **Local Unitary Update:** Tier-0 dynamics are deterministic, reversible, and forward-evolving.
2. **Causal Cone Closure:** Influence propagates only within closed causal cones.
3. **Non-Injective Projection:** Observation maps many micro-histories to a single macro-description.
4. **Boundary-Mediated Records:** Observable facts are exported only through boundary cuts.

Because projection occurs *after* micro-evolution, late choices of observable algebra do not modify past dynamics; they only change which equivalence classes are used to summarize them.

---

### Consequences

- Apparent retrocausality (e.g. delayed-choice experiments) does **not** imply backward-in-time influence.
- Multiple observer narratives may coexist, even if mutually incompatible, so long as their boundary records do not collide.
- Decoherence arises precisely when incompatible records are forced into the same causal cone.
- Horizon complementarity, quantum erasure, and delayed-choice protocols are unified as instances of causal-consistency enforcement.
