## Abstract (1 paragraph)
This paper presents UniNet, a constrained update framework in which locality, unitarity, and finite information transfer force wave-like dynamics and boundary-mediated structure. We show that spacetime geometry, quantum mechanics, and particle structure emerge necessarily as coarse-grained projections of a single underlying update rule. No sector-specific assumptions (QM, GR, SM, Î›CDM) are introduced at the axiomatic level; instead, these frameworks arise as unavoidable macroscopic descriptions under projection.
